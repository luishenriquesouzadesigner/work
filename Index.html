ABOUT ME

I’m a Visual Designer with 8+ years of experience creating high-impact digital and print design solutions across legal, cultural, and corporate environments.


With a postgraduate degree in UX/UI, I specialize in transforming complex information into clear, engaging, and user-centered visual experiences—from institutional presentations and editorial layouts to branding systems and digital products.


I’ve collaborated with multidisciplinary teams to deliver consistent, high-quality outputs across print, web, and social media, always aligned with brand guidelines and business goals.
